About this data

  • Source: USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS) Quick Stats: the quarterly Grain Stocks report (stocks on Mar 1, Jun 1, Sep 1, Dec 1) and the annual Crop Production / Small Grains Summary (production, yield, acres). All wheat classes combined.
  • Gaps: USDA withholds some state values, shown as (D), to avoid disclosing individual farms or elevators, and does not publish every state every quarter. These appear as breaks in the line, never as zero.
  • Forecasts are statistical projections from each state's own history (best of five simple models, chosen by back-testing the last 6 years). The shaded band is an 80% range. When the previous quarter is already published, the next-quarter stocks estimate uses the typical seasonal ratio, which is usually far more accurate. They do not include weather, prices or USDA's own outlook (WASDE). Use them as a baseline, not a guarantee.
  • Updates: USDA publishes Grain Stocks at the end of March, June, September and in mid-January. This page is rebuilt automatically after each release.
